Computational solid mechanics

I develop numerical methods for thin-walled structures — isogeometric and meshfree formulations for plates and shells made of advanced materials — and use them to study large deflection, buckling and postbuckling. A second line of work brings deep learning into the nonlinear solver itself and into structural engineering practice.

Research

Research interests

IGA · NURBS

Isogeometric analysis of plates and shells

Exact-geometry NURBS discretisations for thin and moderately thick shells, membranes and ultra-thin structures, including form finding and large-deflection analysis.

Nonlinear · Stability

Buckling and postbuckling

Geometrically nonlinear response of imperfect shells under axial and combined loading; tracing of limit points and snap-through.

FGM · CNTRC · TPMS

Advanced and architected materials

Functionally graded, carbon-nanotube-reinforced, graphene-platelet and triply periodic minimal surface structures, including porosity and sandwich layouts.

Meshfree

Moving Kriging meshfree methods

Improved moving Kriging formulations with refined and quasi-3D theories for static, vibration and nonlinear bending analysis.

Data-driven solvers

Deep learning inside nonlinear solvers

Learned predictors that shorten Newton–Raphson iteration, time-series forecasting of load paths, and coarse-mesh analysis corrected by recurrent networks.

AI in construction

Machine learning for structural engineering

Defect detection in concrete, masonry, tunnels and sewers; capacity prediction of structural members; explainable AI for engineering decisions.
